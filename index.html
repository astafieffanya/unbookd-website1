The face behind unbookd.

Hi, I'm Anya.

.

My experience in the event industry started on the other side of the booking process: behind the decks.

As an international DJ and music producer from Luxembourg, I've performed at events for brands including Louis Vuitton, Cartier, Porsche and CUPRA. Through these experiences, I gained first-hand insight into how event bookings work behind the scenes, and how important music is in shaping the atmosphere of an event.

I also noticed a recurring issue: the client's vision doesn't always make it all the way to the artist. Communication passes between different people, details get lost, and the musical direction can become disconnected from what the client originally imagined.

I founded unbookd. to change that.

The idea is simple: to create a more direct connection between the client, the music and the artist. We take the time to understand the event, its identity and the atmosphere the client wants to create. From there, we carefully curate the musical direction, select the right artist and make sure every detail is clearly communicated, from the initial brief to the final set.

With one point of contact throughout the process, unbookd. makes music booking more personal, considered and seamless.

Because finding the right DJ is only part of it. The real difference is making sure the music feels right for the moment.
